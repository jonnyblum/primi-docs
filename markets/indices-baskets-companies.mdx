Primi offers three levels of market exposure: a single composite index for the entire private market, six thematic sector baskets, and individual company indices. All three use the same momentum methodology and launch at 100.

---

## The Primi Index (PIX)

PIX is a single composite index measuring the aggregate momentum of the large private company ecosystem. It is calculated as an equal-weighted average of all listed individual company Primi indices where data quality meets the minimum threshold.

PIX launches at 100 and rises when the average listed company is beating its own momentum baseline, and falls when the average company is decelerating relative to its history. It is the pulse of the entire private market in one number — the same way the S&P 500 represents large-cap public equities, or VIX represents the pulse of market volatility.

PIX is updated daily and is the primary reference for broad private market exposure without picking individual companies.

## Sector Baskets

Primi offers six thematic sector baskets, each calculated as an equal-weighted average of the individual company Primi indices within that sector. Each basket launches at 100 and moves based on the collective excess momentum of its member companies relative to their individual 12-month baselines.

| Basket | Companies |
|---|---|
| **AI** | OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Thinking Machines Lab, Cohere, SandboxAQ, Groq, Anysphere |
| **Fintech** | Stripe, Plaid, Brex, Ramp, Rippling |
| **Space & Defense** | SpaceX, Anduril, Physical Intelligence, Figure AI |
| **Consumer** | Fanatics, Discord, Epic Games, Canva, Impossible Foods |
| **Data & Infrastructure** | Databricks, Scale AI, Glean, xAI |
| **Crypto** | Kraken |

Sector baskets allow traders to express a view on an entire theme — the AI sector accelerating or the fintech sector decelerating — without picking individual names.

## Individual Company Indices

Each of the listed companies has its own continuous momentum index that launches at 100 on its listing date and updates daily.

The index measures whether that company's secondary market bid-ask momentum is accelerating or decelerating relative to its own trailing 12-month baseline — not the absolute price level or the rate of appreciation in isolation.

- A **rising index** means the company is appreciating faster than its own recent history.
- A **falling index** means it is decelerating below its own baseline, even if the absolute secondary market price is still rising.

This structure creates a genuinely two-sided market for every listed company. Even the most obviously successful private companies have uncertain near-term momentum relative to their own history.

Each index is calculated from bid-ask midpoints aggregated across independent secondary market platforms using a published, transparent methodology reproducible by any third party.
