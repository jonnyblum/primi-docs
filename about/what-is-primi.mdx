Primi is the first regulated US exchange that lets you trade the momentum and trajectory of the world's largest private companies — without owning shares.

Over the last decade, the world's most exciting financial growth has moved behind closed doors. Companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, and SpaceX are worth more, growing faster, and staying private longer than ever before. Yet everyday investors remain locked out of this trajectory entirely.

Primi changes that.

## What Primi offers

We aggregate live bid-ask quotes from established pre-IPO secondary markets — including Forge, Hiive, and Nasdaq Private Market — and calculate a continuous, transparent momentum index for each listed company. On top of that index, we offer perpetual-style contracts that anyone can trade.

Each listed company has its own index, its own price, and its own market. Positions can be opened or closed at any time. Gains and losses are determined solely by how the index moves between entry and exit.

## What Primi is not

Primi markets are **cash-settled** and do not confer ownership, equity rights, dividends, voting rights, or any direct claim on a company's assets or financial performance. You are not buying shares. You are not investing in the company. You are expressing a view on the direction and momentum of its secondary market activity.

The index for each company is calculated by an independent index provider from live bid-ask quotes and verified transactions on established secondary platforms. Every price move requires real capital and is subject to transparent market rules, position limits, and ongoing surveillance.

## The bigger picture

Over time, Primi's markets will create the first continuous public signal for how the world perceives private companies — the same function that public equity markets serve for listed companies. We are building the stock market for companies not on the stock market.
