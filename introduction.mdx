## Overview

Primi is a market intelligence platform focused on transparent, methodology-driven indices. We track a curated set of companies and provide clear, consistent data so that investors, researchers, and enthusiasts can follow market performance with confidence.

Our indices are built on a rules-based approach — every inclusion decision, weighting scheme, and rebalancing event follows documented criteria. There are no black boxes.

## What We Track

Primi currently covers **30 companies** across key sectors. Each company is evaluated against our index criteria before inclusion and is subject to ongoing review.

## Why Primi?

| | Traditional Indices | Primi |
|---|---|---|
| **Methodology** | Often opaque | Fully documented |
| **Rebalancing** | Quarterly or annual | Rules-based, event-driven |
| **Coverage** | Broad, passive | Curated, active criteria |
| **Data access** | Limited | CSV export, charts, data sources |

## Data Freshness

Market data on the Primi platform is updated regularly. All price data and index values reflect end-of-day calculations unless otherwise noted.
