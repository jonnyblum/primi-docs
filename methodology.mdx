## Overview

Primi indices follow a transparent, rules-based methodology. This page documents the criteria used for company inclusion, how weights are assigned, and when indices are rebalanced.

<Info>
  All methodology decisions are applied consistently. Changes to the methodology are versioned and documented.
</Info>

## Inclusion Criteria

A company must meet all of the following criteria to be eligible for inclusion in a Primi index:

1. **Listing** — The company must be listed on a major exchange (NYSE, NASDAQ, or equivalent).
2. **Liquidity** — Minimum average daily trading volume threshold must be met.
3. **Market Capitalization** — The company must meet the minimum float-adjusted market cap requirement for the relevant index tier.
4. **Financial Reporting** — The company must have at least two consecutive quarters of audited financial statements available.

## Weighting Scheme

Primi indices use a **modified float-adjusted market capitalization** weighting approach.

- Each constituent's weight is based on its float-adjusted market cap relative to the total index market cap.
- A **single-constituent cap** of 20% is applied to prevent any one company from dominating the index.
- Weights are recalculated at each rebalancing event.

### Weight Calculation

```
Weight(i) = FloatCap(i) / Sum(FloatCap) × 100
```

After applying the 20% cap, excess weight is redistributed proportionally across remaining constituents.

## Rebalancing

Indices are rebalanced on a **rules-based schedule**:

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Quarterly review | Full eligibility review; additions and removals applied |
| Corporate action | Spin-offs, mergers, delistings trigger immediate review |
| Weight drift | If any constituent breaches the 20% cap between reviews, an off-cycle rebalance is triggered |

Rebalancing takes effect at the market open on the announced effective date.

## Index Calculation

Index values are calculated using the **Laspeyres formula**:

```
Index Value = (Sum of Adjusted Prices × Shares) / Divisor
```

The divisor is adjusted for corporate actions and constituent changes to maintain index continuity.

## Corporate Actions

| Event | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Stock split | Divisor adjusted; no index discontinuity |
| Dividend | Price-return index does not adjust; total-return index reinvests |
| Merger / acquisition | Constituent removed at close on effective date |
| Spin-off | Evaluated for independent inclusion eligibility |

## Data Sources

Primi sources market data from established financial data providers. All source data is validated before being incorporated into index calculations.
